Training

eGP training: learn to bid on government tenders yourself.

A hands-on half-day workshop on the government eGP portal, led by a certified eGP trainer. Bring your laptop and leave knowing how to find, prepare and submit a tender.

What you'll learn

  1. 1

    Register and update your eGP profile

    Company details, directors, documents and AGPO status.

  2. 2

    Find the right tenders

    Search, filter by category and download tender documents.

  3. 3

    Prepare a complete bid

    The documents procuring entities ask for and how to organise them.

  4. 4

    Submit on eGP

    Uploading, pricing, bid security and getting proof of submission.

  5. 5

    Avoid disqualification

    The common mistakes that knock good bids out at evaluation.

  6. 6

    After you win

    Award, contract, LPO, delivery and getting paid.

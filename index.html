Case study · Tender Tracker

We run our own tender desk on it.

Before
Tender notices, bid documents and certificate expiry dates were spread across email, Excel and chat threads. A late document could cost a bid.
What we built
One tracker for every bid. It has readiness checks, team tasks, director sign-off before submission, and alerts before Tax, AGPO and NCA certificates expire.
Result
The whole team sees the same picture, and the director approves submissions from a phone. Clients get the same system with their own logo.

Kenyan rules built in. KRA VAT and PAYE, SHA, NSSF, Housing Levy, AGPO, NCA and public procurement steps are part of the systems from day one. You don't have to set them up yourself.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you supply to county governments as well as national agencies?

Yes. We have served county governments, national agencies, hospitals and state corporations, including Homa Bay County Government, KenGen and Kenyatta National Hospital.

Are you registered under AGPO?

Yes. Payron Agency is an AGPO Youth registered supplier. We can share our registration and compliance documents with procuring entities on request.

Where is my business data stored?

In your own Google account. You own the data and keep full backups, and we can hand everything over at any time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Your team opens one secure link on any phone or computer, and it can be added to the home screen like an app.

What does monthly support cover?

Small changes, updates and fixes, and help for your team. Bigger features are quoted separately before we start. The first month is free, and paying yearly gets you 2 months free.

Can we start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with one system and add others at any time. They join the same login for your team.

Can you connect M-Pesa or KRA eTIMS?

M-Pesa payments are available as an add-on. eTIMS integration is available on request, and we'll scope it with you before quoting.

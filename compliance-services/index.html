Advisory & Compliance Services

Government paperwork, handled for you.

Renewals, registrations and statutory filings take time away from running your business. We handle them, track the expiry dates, and remind you before anything lapses.

  • Business & Company Registration

    Business names and limited companies, including companies with foreign shareholders. CR12 applications, changes of directors and officials, and official searches with BRS.

  • AGPO Applications

    New AGPO applications and renewals for youth, women and persons with disabilities, so you can bid for reserved tenders.

  • eGP Registration & Training

    Supplier registration on the government eGP portal, plus hands-on training for your team from a certified eGP trainer.

  • KRA Services

    PIN registration, VAT registration, VAT, PAYE and rental income returns, tax compliance certificates, eTIMS activation and tax advice.

  • NCA Services

    Contractor registration, category upgrades and annual renewals with the National Construction Authority.

  • SHA Services

    Employer and member registration with the Social Health Authority, and monthly contributions.

  • NSSF Services

    Employer registration, member registration and monthly returns.

  • NTSA Services

    Vehicle transfers, logbook searches, linking your company to its NTSA profile, and other eCitizen transport services.

  • Visa & Passport Services

    Visa and passport applications: forms, supporting documents and submission.

  • Quotations & Supplier Registration

    Submitting IFMIS quotations, and registering your company as a supplier with ministries, counties and state agencies.

  • Business Printing

    Delivery note books, invoice books, business cards and letterheads, designed and printed.

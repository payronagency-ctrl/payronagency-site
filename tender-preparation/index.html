01 Tender Preparation

Your tender, prepared right and submitted on time.

Most bids are lost on paperwork, not price. We prepare complete, compliant tender documents for your company, from reading the tender notice to uploading on the eGP portal before the deadline. You get one person handling your bid from start to finish.

What we do

  1. Tender search

    We find open tenders in your line of business and send them to you with the closing dates.

  2. Bid document preparation

    We fill in every form, schedule and price sheet, and put together your company documents in the order the tender asks for.

  3. Compliance check

    Before submission, we check every mandatory requirement so your bid is not disqualified at the opening stage.

  4. eGP submission

    We upload and submit your bid on the government eGP portal and send you the submission confirmation.

  5. Prequalification and registration

    We prepare your documents for supplier prequalification with ministries, counties and state agencies.

How it works

Four steps from tender notice to proof of submission.

Payment terms

  1. 75%deposit to start
  2. 25%on handover
  3. 1st monthof support free on every package
  4. 2 monthsfree when support is paid yearly

Example on Business: KES 63,750 deposit, KES 21,250 on handover, then KES 5,000 a month from month two. All prices are in Kenya shillings and include 16% VAT.

What support covers

  • Fixes if anything stops working
  • Small changes: new fields, labels, report tweaks, adding or removing staff
  • Help for your team by WhatsApp or phone, Monday to Friday
  • A monthly check that your backups are in place
  • New features or modules are quoted separately before any work starts

Terms in plain words

  • Handover means the system is live in your Google account, your team is trained and you have the manual.
  • You own your system and your data. If you stop support, the system keeps running; you just lose fixes and changes.
  • Cancel support any time with 30 days' notice. Yearly support is not refunded part-way.
  • Prices are fixed for the first 12 months of support.

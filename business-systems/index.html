Case study · Tender Tracker

We run our own tender desk on it.

Before
Tender notices, bid documents and certificate expiry dates were spread across email, Excel and chat threads. A late document could cost a bid.
What we built
One tracker for every bid. It has readiness checks, team tasks, director sign-off before submission, and alerts before Tax, AGPO and NCA certificates expire.
Result
The whole team sees the same picture, and the director approves submissions from a phone. Clients get the same system with their own logo.

Case study · Business Management System

Business Management System for a Nairobi general supplies company

Before
Tenders, orders, stock, supplier prices and money in and out were tracked separately, and the director had no single view of the business.
What we built
One system covering tenders, orders, BOQs, stock checks, pricing, suppliers, money in and out, daily staff tasks and profit and loss charts. Each staff member has their own login with access limited to their work.
Result
Live and in daily use on the company's laptops, with the director seeing sales, stock and profit in one place.

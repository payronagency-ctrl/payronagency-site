Business registration

Register your business name or limited company.

We register business names and private limited companies with the Business Registration Service on eCitizen, then get you the KRA PIN, CR12 and documents you need to open a bank account and start bidding for tenders.

Ask on WhatsApp Get a Quote

Which one do you need?

  • Business name

    For a sole trader or partnership. Quick and simple, but the owner is personally responsible for the business.

  • Private limited company

    A separate legal entity with directors and shareholders. Owners’ liability is limited.

  • Changes and searches

    CR12 requests, changes of directors, officials and shareholding, and official searches.

Not sure which suits you? We explain the difference for your situation before you decide.

How we do it

What you'll need, and what we do.

  • Three proposed names, in order of preference
  • ID and KRA PIN of every owner, director and shareholder
  • Passport photos, email address and phone number of each
  • Physical and postal address of the business
  • Shareholding split and business activity (companies)
  1. 1

    Name search

    We check and reserve your preferred name with BRS.

  2. 2

    Registration

    We prepare and submit the registration on eCitizen, including beneficial ownership details for companies.

  3. 3

    Certificates

    You receive the registration certificate or certificate of incorporation, and the CR12 for companies.

  4. 4

    Next steps

    Company KRA PIN, then AGPO and eGP registration if you plan to bid for tenders.

What is a CR12?

The official record of a company’s directors and shareholders. Most tenders and banks ask for a recent one.

Can foreigners be shareholders?

Yes. We register companies with foreign shareholders and directors.

What do I need after registering to bid for tenders?

A KRA PIN and Tax Compliance Certificate, a business permit, eGP registration and, if you qualify, an AGPO certificate. We can do all of them.

Can you change directors on an existing company?

Yes, along with officials, shareholding and the registered office.

About Payron Agency

One office for your tenders, compliance, supplies and systems.

A Nairobi firm working in Kenya's public procurement sector since 2018.

Who we are

Payron Agency is a Nairobi firm that has worked in Kenya's public procurement sector since 2018. We started by bidding for and supplying government institutions ourselves. Today we use that experience to help other businesses win tenders, stay compliant and run their operations on proper systems.

We are an AGPO Youth registered supplier, and our team includes a certified eGP trainer. We know the procurement process from both sides: as a bidder and as the firm that prepares the bid.

What we deal with

AreaWhat we do
Tender PreparationFinding tenders, preparing complete bid documents, compliance checks, eGP submission and supplier prequalification

Institutional SuppliesFoodstuffs, office supplies, ICT equipment, lab reagents, electrical and industrial materials, fuel and lubricants, delivered against LPOs

KRA services

Your KRA Tax Compliance Certificate, sorted.

Almost every tender asks for a valid Tax Compliance Certificate, and KRA only issues one when your returns are filed and your iTax account is clean. We find what is blocking yours, fix it and apply, then keep your returns on time so it never lapses.

What we handle

  • Registration

    KRA PIN for individuals and companies, and VAT registration.

  • Returns

    Monthly VAT (due by the 20th), PAYE (by the 9th), rental income and annual income tax returns.

  • Certificates and eTIMS

    Tax Compliance Certificate applications, and eTIMS set-up for your invoices.

A Tax Compliance Certificate is valid for 12 months. We remind you before it expires.

How we do it

What you'll need, and what we do.

  • Your KRA PIN and iTax login (or you log in with us)
  • ID of the taxpayer or directors
  • Sales and purchase records for VAT returns
  • Payroll details for PAYE
  • Any letters or notices you have received from KRA
  1. 1

    Check iTax

    We look at your account for missing returns, unpaid tax and penalties.

  2. 2

    Fix it

    We file what is missing and show you exactly what, if anything, needs paying.

  3. 3

    Apply

    We apply for the Tax Compliance Certificate and send it to you.

  4. 4

    Monthly returns filed on time, with a reminder before the certificate expires.

Why was my Tax Compliance Certificate application declined?

Usually unfiled returns, unpaid tax or penalties on iTax. We find the cause and clear it.

How long is a Tax Compliance Certificate valid?

12 months from the date it is issued.

I have never filed returns. Can you help?

Yes. We file the missing returns, including nil returns where you had no income, and bring the account up to date.

Do you file VAT every month?

Yes. Many of our clients leave their monthly VAT and PAYE with us, so the certificate stays valid all year.

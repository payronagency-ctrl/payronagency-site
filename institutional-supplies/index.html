How LPO deliveries work

From LPO to invoice, with matching paperwork.

  1. 1

    Send the LPO

    Share your LPO, tender award or item specifications on WhatsApp or email.

  2. 2

    Confirm and source

    We confirm quantities, prices and the delivery date, then source the goods.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    We deliver to your stores with a delivery note that matches the LPO.

  4. 4

    Invoice

    You receive an invoice that matches the LPO and delivery note, ready for payment processing.

NCA registration

NCA contractor registration, upgrades and renewals.

Anyone carrying on business as a contractor in Kenya must be registered with the National Construction Authority and hold a current practising licence. We handle new registrations, category upgrades and the yearly renewal, so your licence is valid when the tender asks for it.

Categories and classes

  • Eight categories

    NCA1 (largest contracts) down to NCA8 (smallest). Your category sets the contract value you can take on.

  • Classes of work

    Building, civil engineering, electrical, mechanical and specialist works. You can register in several classes.

  • Annual licence

    The practising licence is renewed every year. Late renewal attracts penalties.

We advise on the right category for your experience, staff and finances, so the application is not downgraded or rejected.

How we do it

What you'll need, and what we do.

  • Certificate of incorporation or business registration, and CR12
  • KRA PIN and a valid Tax Compliance Certificate
  • IDs of directors
  • Academic and professional certificates and CVs of your technical staff
  • Proof of financial capability, such as bank statements
  • List of equipment and past projects
  • The relevant EPRA licence for electrical contractors
  1. 1

    Choose category and class

    We match your staff, equipment and finances to the right NCA category.

  2. 2

    Prepare the file

    We put together the documents and staff details NCA evaluates.

  3. 3

    Apply and pay

    We submit on the NCA portal and guide you through the official fees.

  4. 4

    Licence and renewals

    We follow up until the certificate and practising licence are issued, then remind you before each renewal.

Good to know.

How often do I renew?

Every year. Renewing late costs more, so we diary yours.

Can I move to a higher category later?

Yes. When your experience and finances grow, we apply for an upgrade.

Can I register for more than one class?

Yes, for example building and civil works. You hold one category per class.

Do I need NCA registration for tenders?

For construction tenders, yes: procuring entities ask for a valid NCA certificate and practising licence.

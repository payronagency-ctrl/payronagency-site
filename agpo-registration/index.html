AGPO registration

Get your AGPO certificate and bid for reserved government tenders.

The government reserves 30% of its procurement for businesses owned by youth, women and persons with disabilities. An AGPO certificate is your ticket to those tenders. We check you qualify, prepare every document and follow the application until your certificate is issued.

Ask on WhatsApp Get a Quote

Who qualifies

  • Youth

    Owners and directors aged 18 to 35.

  • Women

    Women-owned and women-led businesses, any age.

  • Persons with disabilities

    Owners registered with the National Council for Persons with Disabilities.

The business must be at least 70% owned by the group, and its leadership must be 100% from that group.

How we do it

What you'll need, and what we do.

  • National ID or passport of every owner and director
  • Business registration certificate or certificate of incorporation
  • CR12 for limited companies, or partnership deed for partnerships
  • KRA PIN and a valid Tax Compliance Certificate
  • NCPWD registration (persons with disabilities)
  • NCA certificate (construction businesses)
  1. 1

    Eligibility check

    We confirm ownership and leadership meet the AGPO rules before anything is filed.

  2. 2

    Documents

    We collect and check your documents. If your Tax Compliance Certificate has expired, we renew it first.

  3. 3

    Application

    We complete and submit the application on the AGPO portal.

  4. 4

    Certificate

    We follow up until it is issued, send you a copy and diary the renewal date.

Good to know.

Does the government charge for AGPO registration?

No. AGPO registration is free. Our fee covers checking eligibility, preparing the documents and following the application through.

How long is an AGPO certificate valid?

Two years. We remind you before it expires so you never miss a reserved tender.

Can I still bid for open tenders?

Yes. An AGPO certificate adds the reserved tenders to everything you can already bid for.

My Tax Compliance Certificate has expired. Can I still apply?

You need a valid one first. We sort out the KRA side, then apply for AGPO.
